# Josaa98 brand assets

- `josaa98-original.png`: supplied original, retained unchanged.
- `josaa98-logo.png`: high-resolution PNG reproduction generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.
- `josaa98-logo-horizontal.png`: transparent horizontal lockup for the compact website header.
- `josaa98-logo-horizontal-light.png`: white wordmark and subtitle for the dark footer, preserving the orange numerals and colored emblem.
- `josaa98-social-flyer.png`: 1200 × 630 PNG social sharing flyer.
- `icon-32.png`, `icon-48.png`, `icon-180.png`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`: size derivatives of the logo for browser and app icons.

The original remains the reference for the official artwork. The logo reproduction is generated, rather than a pixel-exact enlargement.

## Generation prompts

Logo: Reproduce the supplied official JOSAA98 logo at high resolution. Preserve the navy background, blue and orange buildings, crane and suspended load, roof swoosh, window, custom lettering and underline ornament. Text: “JOSAA98” and “CONSTRUCTION AND CIVIL WORKS”. Change only resolution and edge clarity; no redesign or extra elements.

Flyer: Landscape social sharing flyer with the complete supplied logo on the left and the title “Building with quality & reliability.” on the right. Supporting copy: “Residential • Commercial • Civil Works”, “Kumasi, Ghana”, “0249778376”, and the website. Navy, blue, orange and white palette with generous safe margins.

Final flyer edit: Change only the website text from “josaa98.com” to “josaa98.org”, preserving the other artwork and text.

Header logo: Adapt the supplied logo into a compact horizontal lockup on a transparent background. Keep the blue-and-orange building and crane emblem on the left, the original JOSAA98 lettering on the right, and the company subtitle below the lettering. Remove the background, corner accent and long underline ornament. Preserve the brand style and colors.

Footer logo: Change only the blue JOSAA lettering and subtitle to white. Preserve the orange 98, colored emblem, typography, layout and transparent background.

## Sharing metadata

The production origin is `https://josaa98.org`. Open Graph and Twitter card metadata are in `index.html` so crawlers can read them without running JavaScript. Deploy the built `dist` directory to that origin before checking public link previews. Previously cached previews may require refreshing through the platform's sharing debugger.
